אם הטלפון יאבד בלי גיבוי שמיש, הרישומים יאבדו איתו. אין לנו עותק לשחזר ממנו. שחזור מקובץ מחזיר רק את מה שהיה בו כשהוא נוצר.

Privacy

The app does not send your ledger to us. Records are stored on your device.

We do not receive your children's names, your balances, or anything you write down. There is no account or sign-up, and we do not hold a database of your family’s records.

What is also true

  • The app stores. Apple and Google tell us how many people installed the app and send us a report when it crashes. That is part of distributing through them and we cannot switch all of it off. We do not combine this information with family ledger records.
  • Contacting support. You can email us at metoraf.berserk@gmail.com. If you email us we will have your address and whatever you wrote. Please describe what happened rather than sending a picture of your child's page.

Privacy and support website

When you open the hosted version of this page, its host, GitHub (through GitHub Pages), receives and logs technical request information, such as your IP address, visit time and requested page, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. GitHub may process and store this information in the United States. We do not have access to these logs, and we do not set up any analytics. How GitHub handles this information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app's ledger: opening the website does not send it your family's records.

Backup

The ledger can be included in your phone backup if backup is enabled and includes the app. That copy is held by Apple or Google under your account, not by us. Installing the app does not guarantee that a backup has been made.

You can also export a backup file and choose where to save it. Without a passphrase, anyone who opens it can read the records. A passphrase encrypts the file, but we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Opening the share sheet does not confirm that the file was saved; check the chosen destination.

If the phone is lost without a usable backup, the records are lost with it. We have no copy to restore. A file restores only the records it contained when it was created.

Deleting

Deleting the app and its data removes the ledger from the device. Exported files and backups under your Apple or Google account are managed separately; deleting the app does not delete those copies. We have no ledger copy to delete on your behalf.

Children

This is a product for families. A child never signs up alone and we collect nothing about them — no email address, no photograph, no date of birth, no location. A parent manages the ledger on their device and can delete the app data. Backup copies and exported files are managed separately, as explained above.

Money

No real money ever moves. There is no bank connection, no card, no transfer. It is a record of the money a parent is holding for their children — like a note on the fridge, with interest calculations and a history of changes.

This covers the free version, where the ledger is managed on one device. If and when a connected version with an account and a server exists, it will have its own, longer policy published before it is available.
